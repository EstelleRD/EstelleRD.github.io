Projects

A data-driven identification of brain phenotypes determinants: highlighting the broad and dominant influences of blood pressure and cardiovascular factors

2023 · Master thesis, Harvard Medical School · Supervised by Chirag Patel and Jacques Fellay

A comprehensive, data-driven mapping of the factors that shape brain structure and function, testing 3,912 MRI imaging-derived phenotypes against a broad range of physiological, environmental, lifestyle, medical, and genetic predictors in 43,593 UK Biobank participants. Across ~1.8 million associations, blood pressure and cardiovascular factors — diastolic and systolic blood pressure in particular — emerged as the most broadly and consistently influential, especially on white-matter integrity.
